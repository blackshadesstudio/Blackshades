What you share

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, shoot type, and message. A preferred date and location are optional. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your message.

How it is used

Your enquiry information is used to respond to your request and discuss the photography services you are interested in. The form does not subscribe you to a marketing list.

Where it goes

When this website is hosted on Netlify with Forms enabled, enquiries are processed by Netlify Forms and made available to the studio through its hosting account. Hosting services may also process technical request information needed to serve and protect the website.

External links

WhatsApp and Instagram links open those services, while phone and email links open your device’s calling or email app. Information you share through these services is subject to their own privacy practices. This site does not embed social posts or load third-party analytics scripts.

Questions about your information

To ask about an enquiry, correct your details, or request that your enquiry be deleted, email blackshadesstudio@gmail.com. You can also contact the studio on WhatsApp or call +971 54 484 0241.
